# Ladrok Launcher

The home screen of Ladrok TV: the first thing on screen after boot, from which the viewer opens everything else. It follows the Ladrok mantra: only what you need, nothing that shouts, little to set up.

## Language

**Launcher**:
The Ladrok TV home screen. It is the only home screen on the device.
_Avoid_: Home app, start screen, dashboard

**App**:
Something installed on the TV that declares itself as a TV app and can be opened from the Launcher.
_Avoid_: Application, program, channel

**Tile**:
An App's place on the Keypad. Each App has exactly one Tile.
_Avoid_: Card, icon, banner

**Keypad**:
The right-hand part of the Launcher: Tiles in pages of nine, three by three like an old phone's keypad, each with a digit that opens it.
_Avoid_: App grid, app drawer, dock

**Program**:
One thing to watch in Watch Next or a Channel, which opens in its App.
_Avoid_: Item, card, content, show

**Settings Tile**:
The Tile that opens the system settings, always the last one. It is part of the Launcher itself; the system Settings App has no Tile of its own, only lends the Settings Tile its banner.
_Avoid_: Settings button, gear

**Watch Next**:
The system-wide list of Programs the viewer started and can continue, published by the Apps themselves. The first row on the left of the Launcher when not empty.
_Avoid_: Continue watching, recents, history

**Channel**:
A named list of Programs that an App publishes for the home screen (for example "Trending"). Hidden until the viewer switches it on from that App's Tile. Not a broadcast TV channel.
_Avoid_: Preview channel, feed, recommendations

**Move**:
The viewer rearranging Tiles by hand. It is the only way the order changes.
_Avoid_: Reorder, sort, pin
